# Mid-distance off-track dressing concepts R1

Created 2026-09-25 with the built-in image-generation tool. Nine low-detail silhouette ideas across three sheets. Concept art only; no game implementation or tests.

- [Rings](15-mid-distance-rings-r1.png): R1 Offset halos; R2 Open cradles; R3 Tilted pair.
- [Pylons](15-mid-distance-pylons-r1.png): P1 Forked masts; P2 Leaning buttresses; P3 Stacked piers.
- [Side structures](15-mid-distance-side-structures-r1.png): S1 Radiator fins; S2 Pipe loops; S3 Service blades.

Intent: fill the middle distance between roadside furniture and remote stations. Broad ivory/graphite forms, restrained steel/brass, sparse white lights, generous course clearance. Approximate distances in prompts are conceptual, not validated placement specifications. Suggested starting combination: R2, P2 and S1. No selection is assumed.

The generated images add rocky foundations and asteroid density that are not requirements. Some structures appear closer to the course than requested; implementation should establish clear separation using actual course dimensions. R1's rear hoop can read as a gate, so use off-axis placement. Preserve only useful silhouette/material ideas. Full ring outlines are partly cropped by the panoramic panels.

## Exact prompts

### Rings

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: concept-art selection sheet for mid-distance OFF-TRACK environment dressing in an orbital anti-gravity racing game. Create a landscape image with three wide horizontal panels stacked vertically, one distinct environment option per panel. High-quality believable 3D game concept art but intentionally SIMPLE MODELABLE FORMS: broad untextured plates, chunky beams, very few seams, no tiny greebles, no dense machinery or rivets. Warm ivory armor over graphite structure, brushed steel and tiny restrained brass accents. Sparse tiny steady pearl-white practical lights, warm sun and cool fill, navy space. A broad graphite racetrack ribbon with ivory edges curves from foreground toward the distance in each panel; camera is an elevated three-quarter concept-art viewpoint, with enough track visible to clearly judge separation and relative scale. Decorations sit about 2–5 road widths beyond the road edges; much closer than remote space stations but not road furniture. Large recognizable silhouettes, significant empty space between structures. No vehicles, HUD, characters, glowing energy curtains, neon rings, arrows, checkpoint markings, dominant planets, space stations, microtexture or busy backgrounds. Keep racing corridor and sightlines open. Short crisp panel label in upper left margin, never printed on objects. This is a focused concept exploration of scene dressing, not finished gameplay. Subject: OVERSIZED RING STRUCTURES around or beside the course, spare monumental infrastructure, clearly mechanical and inactive. Panel label "R1 — OFFSET HALOS": two immense thick segmented circular hoops, staggered and canted beside the road, each diameter 5–7 road widths, their open centers face obliquely toward camera, road passes outside them with clear separation. Panel "R2 — OPEN CRADLES": three huge incomplete C-shaped ivory ring ribs loosely wrapping around the course environment at long intervals, open at the top and upper right; broad chunky structural arcs located several road widths away, track a small ribbon threading through enormous empty space. Show open broken circumference prominently so they do not look like checkpoints. Panel "R3 — TILTED PAIR": two differently tilted oversized thin mechanical hoops connected to a single remote chunky graphite anchor spine below and to one side of the road; rings mostly beside and below road elevation, simple six-part ivory cladding, clear off-track gap. Prioritize varied silhouettes and very low detail. Do not place repeated hoops tightly over the road.
```
