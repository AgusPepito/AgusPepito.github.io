# Encounter test guide — lab 009

The start menu offers four isolated encounters and the existing highway run. Weapons autofire; existing PC/mobile cruise, fast, brake and boost controls apply to every choice. Retry preserves the selected encounter. The header, pause screen and results screen provide a route back to selection. Practice mode remains available in Tune. Enemy models and effects are temporary code-authored primitives.

| Encounter | Mechanic | Current tuning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dart squadron | First test is one squad of six, two per role. Red ships fire three-shot bursts at your position captured when preparation begins. Cyan ships lead your lateral motion, capped at six metres; stopping/reversing defeats their prediction. Gold ships fire three-bullet fans. Preparation is shared, firing staggered, and firing order rotates between volleys. Role strips, warning lights and bullet colors identify each attack. | 3 HP each, 24 m/s; V/column formation changes retained; 2.8 s attack cycle, 0.7 s minimum preparation; direct/predictive/spread bullets 30/36/28 m/s. No homing. |
| Interceptors | Three rivals stage farther ahead and alternate a complete charge → side-dash warning → side dash → exposed engine sequence. The dash direction locks when its warning begins. Only one attack chain is active at a time; the next rival prepares during recovery. | 12 HP; armor 20% damage, exposed rear 150%; 0.55 s aim + 0.65 s lock; 0.75 s charge, 0.65 s dash warning, 0.45 s dash across up to 8 m, 1.6 s recovery. No bullets. |
| Orbit mine layers | Two layers drop three-mine clusters with stable, opposite rotation directions. The cluster centers drift along the road. Shooting a mine leaves a moving gap; mines do not chain-react. Orbit outlines/arrows show movement; cyan arming rings turn orange when live. Killing the layer leaves its clusters moving. Entire orbits fit within the road and shrink gradually on narrow sections. | Layers 10 HP, 24 m/s; one cluster every 2.2 s per layer; mine arming 1.15 s; orbit radius up to 3.8 m, rotation 0.9 rad/s, forward drift 6 m/s; 96 active mines maximum. |
| Armed convoy | Escorts are removed for this test. Two independently destructible rear-corner turrets alternate tracking and sweeping bursts. The truck signals lane changes, and breaking a lock triggers a warned defensive fan from surviving turrets. Shoot weapons for safety or locks for salvage; opening all three removes the hull and remaining turrets. | Turrets 10 HP; tracking burst six shots, sweep seven shots, 30 m/s bullets; normal warning 0.65 s, defensive warning 0.8 s. Locks 16 HP. Lane-change warning 0.9 s, movement 1.6 s. Hauler retains 30/18 m/s speed changes. |

Cruise matches the hauler when close behind, giving time to work across the locks. Fast/boost bypass following. Interceptors pace ahead while warning so maximum speed cannot consume the reaction window. Mines have swept proximity checks at boost speed; unarmed mines allow contact without damage.

Results distinguish **Encounter cleared**, **Escaped — objective incomplete**, and **Partial raid**. Remaining relevant mines/pickups must resolve before results. Combat rewards now strongly outweigh passing: Dart kills 250, Interceptors 750, layers 600, turrets 500; ordinary passes 10, clean Interceptor passes 75. Opening a lock pays 200; each cyan crate is explicitly labeled **SALVAGE +600** and shows a collection confirmation. A full clear awards a one-time 2,000-point bonus. Fast escape remains an option, not a completed objective.

These are isolated behavior tests, not yet mixed into the highway ramp schedule. The aim of this revision is to require responses to different targeting/movement behaviors rather than assume that higher projectile counts alone establish difficulty. The user evaluates that result.

Future piercing/spread weapons, ram shield, upgrade rewards, boost recharge pickups and generated 3D assets are not part of this version. Balance and visual readability are for the user's playtest. Agents perform only nonvisual validation, per `AGENTS.md`.
