# Encounter and main-level guide — lab 012

The start menu defaults to **Main level · Mixed highway**: a finite sequence of Darts, Interceptors, mine layers and convoy, followed by a final drive to Finish. The first encounter starts at 120 metres; after resolving a fight, the next station starts at least 160 metres and four seconds later. Health, score and boost persist. Clears retain their bonuses; escapes continue the run without a clear bonus. Mines and salvage resolve before moving to the next encounter. A subway-style route strip shows current/next stations and player position, holding at encounters and advancing during driving breaks. Cleared stations get checks; escapes/partial raids get dashes. The larger final circle represents Finish until a boss exists. Yellow traffic streams during driving breaks; normal red/scout and shield-row reinforcements are reserved for **Original highway**. All four isolated encounter choices remain available. Weapons autofire; existing PC/mobile controls apply to every choice. Retry resets the selected run. Practice mode remains in Tune.

Normal red-car reinforcements were removed from encounters after the lab 011 playtest.

Bypass steering follows the same convention as the main highway: forward motion follows the road, while left/right input changes your position across its lanes. The widening lateral separation from the main road no longer consumes steering speed or pins the car against a moving guardrail. Entry and rejoin preserve lane offset; steering into a wall still causes damage.

**Bypasses in the main level:** take the marked left lane before Darts or the marked right lane before Mine layers. The lane sign appears at least 420 metres before the split, shows remaining distance, and turns green when aligned. Stay in that lane across the painted commit line to enter; staying elsewhere keeps the normal fight. Each shortcut is 560 metres long and nine metres wide, with two dividers marking three lanes. It winds farther outside the tunnel lanes before rejoining. Split and rejoin corridors are placed between same-side tunnel/ramp complexes, with 24 metres of junction clearance. Only the outer guardrail continues through overlapping pavement; the inner guardrail starts after the roads separate. Use steering and braking to avoid its walls. It skips its encounter without kill or clear rewards. The route-map loop turns green and the player marker follows the bypass; the station receives a dash when you rejoin. Fork approaches replace the normal short driving break for those two encounters. No forks are added to the isolated tests or original highway.

Fast-mode pacing: moving traffic and cruising enemies accelerate smoothly with the player, reaching approximately 73–78 m/s against the player's 82 m/s fast speed. Extra boost still reaches 122 m/s while their pacing remains capped at the fast-mode rate, allowing decisive overtaking. Normal-speed values below remain unchanged. This covers yellow traffic, highway scouts/shields, Darts, miners, convoys and the slow part of Interceptor recovery; Interceptor charge/dash behavior and mine/projectile speeds retain their existing rules. Tuning lives in `TRAFFIC_PACE` in `src/simulation.js`.

Dart waves enter from beyond the top/far end of the road instead of appearing in formation. Each ship glides in over 2.4 seconds, staggered by 0.1 seconds per slot, then joins the existing V/column behavior. Arriving Darts do not shoot; the normal visible-shooter and aim-warning rules still apply afterward. This applies to both the main level and the isolated Dart encounter.

Dart identity is permanent: narrow red hulls with three roof dots fire direct bursts; pointed cyan hulls with diamond/crosshair symbols predict movement; wide gold wings with three diverging roof rays fire spreads. A single white chevron marks the reserved next shooter. The current attacker brightens its symbol and weapon while a role-colored ring fills over a 1.2-second preparation. Its muzzle flashes on firing; direct-burst dots dim one per shot and reset at the next preparation. The next-shooter reservation is released if that ship dies or becomes ineligible. HP and bullet patterns remain unchanged; firing preparation was extended from 0.7 to 1.2 seconds for readability. Formation changes no longer light the Dart firing signal.

Original-highway red scouts use 12 m/s bullets with 0.3 radians between neighboring bullets. Each car reloads for 2.8 seconds, and all red cars share a 0.9-second minimum interval between volleys. Shots aim at the player's current position and do not home.

Attacking entities stage approximately 24–39 metres ahead in top-down mode instead of 64–110 metres. Every encounter shot, including scout fire, checks the actual camera projection: the full shooter body must fit within the playable view, with HUD/edge margins, for at least 0.7 seconds. Leaving the view resets that grace period. Interceptors only begin their warning while visible and restart aiming if they leave view before charging. This is a numeric projection gate, not an agent visual review.

Cruise matches the hauler when close behind, giving time to work across the locks. Fast/boost bypass following. Interceptors pace ahead while warning so maximum speed cannot consume the reaction window. Mines have swept proximity checks at boost speed; unarmed mines allow contact without damage.

Results distinguish **Encounter cleared**, **Escaped — objective incomplete**, and **Partial raid**. Remaining relevant mines/pickups must resolve before results. Combat rewards now strongly outweigh passing: Dart kills 250, Interceptors 750, layers 600, turrets 500; ordinary passes 10, clean Interceptor passes 75. Opening a lock pays 200; each cyan crate is explicitly labeled **SALVAGE +600** and shows a collection confirmation. A full clear awards a one-time 2,000-point bonus. Fast escape remains an option, not a completed objective.

Main-level encounters use their existing ahead-of-player staging and visibility gates. Merge lanes now sit below the main road to avoid overlapping surfaces and markings, and their starts have primitive tunnel exits. Original-highway ramp enemies retain their existing entry paths. The user evaluates gameplay and presentation.

Future piercing/spread weapons, ram shield, upgrade rewards, boost recharge pickups and generated 3D assets are not part of this version. Balance and visual readability are for the user's playtest. Agents perform only nonvisual validation, per `AGENTS.md`.
