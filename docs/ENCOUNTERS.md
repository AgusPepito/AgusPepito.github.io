# Encounter test guide — lab 008

The start menu offers four isolated encounters and the existing highway run. Weapons autofire; existing PC/mobile cruise, fast, brake and boost controls apply to every choice. Retry preserves the selected encounter. The header, pause screen and results screen provide a route back to selection. Practice mode remains available in Tune. Enemy models and effects are temporary code-authored primitives.

| Encounter | Mechanic | Current tuning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dart squadron | Three overlapping waves of eight fragile ships, seven seconds apart. Open-center V formations change into staggered columns. Sequential volleys alternate convergence toward a sampled player lane with straight rearward sweeps; bullets never home. Clearing one wave does not end the test. | 3 HP each, 24 m/s; 2.2 s hold, 0.7 s formation warning, 1 s change; volleys every 2.4 s, 0.13 s between shots, 24 m/s bullets. |
| Interceptors | Two rivals alternate tracking warning, locked path, extended charge and short engine recovery. The next attack prepares during the previous recovery, but charges never overlap. A collided rival keeps pace and reengages; a missed charge can be punished or overtaken for 150 points. No bullets. | 12 HP; armor 20% damage, exposed rear 150%; 0.55 s aim + 0.65 s lock, independent of speed; 1.15 s charge including 0.5 s in the attack corridor, 1.6 s recovery. |
| Mine layers | Two layers weave independently and leave overlapping paired trails beyond an initial six-row field. Cyan partial rings mean unarmed; closed orange rings show proximity danger. One shot clears a small pocket, with at most two propagation links. Killing either layer leaves its mines. | 10 HP each, 24 m/s; paired drop every 0.6 s; arm after 1.15 s; blast radius 3.5 m, chain reach 5.5 m, 0.12 s between links; maximum 96 active mines. |
| Convoy hauler | Rear guards alternate aimed and straight three-shot bursts, move across the outer lock firing lines, and warn before switching sides. Hauler alternates acceleration/braking with a brake warning. Three rear locks eject cyan salvage ahead into side lanes; opening all removes the hull. | Hauler 30/18 m/s in a 7 s cycle; locks 16 HP (about 1.28 s focused fire); escorts 8 HP; 0.65 s burst warning, 27 m/s bullets; collected salvage 250 points. |

Cruise matches the hauler when close behind, giving time to work across the locks. Fast/boost bypass following. Interceptors pace ahead while warning so maximum speed cannot consume the reaction window. Mines have swept proximity checks at boost speed; unarmed mines allow contact without damage.

Results distinguish **Encounter cleared**, **Escaped — objective incomplete**, and **Partial raid**. Escaping never awards a clear. Dart tests wait for all three scheduled waves. Remaining relevant mines/pickups must resolve before results. Full engagements aim for sustained pressure rather than instant completion; skilled kills and fast escapes can finish earlier, especially the optional mine/convoy bypasses. The tests use the existing road, including narrowing and bends, with road-relative positions and bounds. They are not yet mixed into the highway ramp schedule.

Future piercing/spread weapons, ram shield, upgrade rewards, boost recharge pickups and generated 3D assets are not part of this version. Balance and visual readability are for the user's playtest. Agents perform only nonvisual validation, per `AGENTS.md`.
